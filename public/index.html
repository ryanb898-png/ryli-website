Live Control

Two tools you run without stopping your stream — one to make the room erupt on cue, one to control everything without ever leaving your Whatnot tab.

🔥Hype Meter PRO

Whatnot is watching your room. Give it something to see.

Whatnot's own engineering team has published that user actions — including chats and reactions — are counted in real time and fed to the service that ranks which livestreams get promoted. In their words, they want to “promote livestreams to our users when the most exciting things are happening at that moment.”

The Hype Meter is how you make that moment happen on cue.

  • Press one key — a bar and a countdown appear on your stream
  • Chat fills it by spamming emojis. The higher it climbs, the bigger the giveaway
  • A live counter shows how many different people are joining in. Whatnot counts distinct users, so that's the number that matters
  • At zero it locks itself in and Ryli announces the prize out loud
  • No giveaway budget? Run it in hype-only mode and just get the room loud

Runs from a hotkey or straight from the RYLI panel inside your Whatnot window — no Stream Deck needed. Whatnot has never published a link between any single action and For You placement, and RYLI never simulates engagement: every tap and message is a real viewer.

🎛️Ryli Companion

Run the whole show without ever leaving it.

While you stream you're watching your own Whatnot page — not the RYLI app in another window. So RYLI puts her controls where you're already looking: a small panel drawn straight into your Whatnot tab. Only you can see it. It's painted locally in your browser, so nothing reaches Whatnot and viewers never see a thing.

  • Launch the Hype Meter, fire a chat announcement, or mute Ryli — one tap, eyes still on your show
  • A green dot on every automation that's currently armed, so you can see what's running at a glance
  • No Stream Deck and nothing to memorise — the buttons do exactly what the hotkeys do
  • Drag it anywhere, collapse it to a single orb, or switch it to a slim bar that stays out of your way
  • If RYLI ever stops responding it greys itself out, rather than showing you a green light that's lying

Included with RYLI. Pro automations stay Pro — but muting Ryli is always free, because you have to be able to silence anything playing over your own show.

🖨️ThermalFlow PRO

Every sale, printed instantly.

The moment a lot sells, ThermalFlow sends a ready-to-pack label straight to your printer — lot number, item, buyer, and price, no keyboard, no breaking your flow.

  • Prints automatically the instant a real sale confirms — no manual entry

  • Also supports compact direct-USB thermal label printers — no driver hunting

Requires a connected label printer. Off by default — turn it on anytime in Settings. Windows only.

Questions, Answered

Before you go live.

Does RYLI replace OBS?
No. RYLI is a desktop companion that works alongside OBS. It gives you live show data, a voice co-host, and a browser-source overlay for your existing stream setup — you keep using OBS exactly as you do today.
Is there a complete setup guide?
Yes — our Complete Setup Guide walks you through OBS, adding a phone camera with DroidCam, and layering RYLI on top, start to finish. No experience required.
Do my viewers need to install anything?
No. Your audience just sees the overlay in your broadcast, same as any other on-screen graphic. RYLI runs entirely on your own computer.
Is my show data private?
Yes. Everything runs locally on your PC. RYLI automatically detects and only logs your own stream — if you raid out or watch someone else's show, logging pauses until your show is back, so your data never mixes with anyone else's.
Is RYLI Whatnot-only?
No — but what you get depends on where you go live. RYLI’s overlay is an OBS browser source, so your themes, camera frame, jumbotron cards, sounds and Ryli’s voice work on Twitch, YouTube, TikTok or anywhere else OBS sends your stream. What is Whatnot-only today is the live data: bids, sales, leaderboards, Insights and Breaker Tools all read from your own Whatnot show. Reading Twitch chat and events is the next connection we are building. Choose “I stream, but don’t sell live” during setup and the sales pages stay out of your way.
Does RYLI work on Twitch?
Your overlay does, today. RYLI is an OBS browser source, so your themes, bezel, camera frame, jumbotron cards, sound packs, ticker and Ryli’s voice all run on Twitch exactly as they do anywhere else. What RYLI cannot do yet is read Twitch chat and events, so leaderboards and celebrations have nothing live to react to. That connection is what we are building next. Pick “I stream, but don’t sell live” in setup and the selling pages disappear.
What are Breaker Tools?
A live board for team breaks, on your stream. Pick a league and every spot arrives with the real team names and colours, or build your own list. As the break runs, each spot shows who landed it — so nobody has to ask who has the Cowboys. You get mini-game signs for Stash or Pass, See 2 Pick 1, Pick Your Team and Random Team, your own team logos on the tiles, and Ryli calling the team out loud the moment a spot settles. Pro, in beta, and it reads your break from your own Whatnot show.
Does RYLI run on Mac?
Yes — through Parallels Desktop, and hosts are already streaming that way. RYLI is a Windows app, so on a Mac it runs inside Parallels while OBS stays native on macOS. RYLI recognises when it's running in Parallels and finds OBS across that boundary on its own, so it's the same one button everyone else presses. Full walkthrough in the Mac section of the setup guide. A native Apple build is on the roadmap, not available yet.
What happens after my free trial?
Your core overlay — themes, leaderboards, milestone celebrations, the announcer, and your all-time/live Insights stats — stays free forever. Your camera feed in the jumbotron is free too. Pro-only features (talking to Ryli, your Top buyers & Suggested outreach boards, premium themes, Ultra FX, premium voices, custom banners, auto auctions and giveaways, label printing) require a subscription to keep using after the 10-day trial.
