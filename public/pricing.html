The bits worth reading twice.

🎁

10 days, no card

Every Pro feature is on from your first launch. Nothing to cancel and nothing to enter — if you do nothing, it simply reverts to free.

🔓

Free is not a trial

When the 10 days end, RYLI keeps running. Pro features switch off; your overlay, your themes, your camera and your stats do not.

A dropped connection won't lock you out

A validated licence is trusted for seven days offline, so a network blip mid-show can never take your tools away.

Billing runs through Polar, who handle the payment and the sales tax. Manage or cancel it from Settings → About, or from Polar's own portal.

One licence covers you, on your machine. Moving to a new PC is a matter of pasting the same key in.
